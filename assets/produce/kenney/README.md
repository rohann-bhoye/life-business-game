# Produce Models

Unmodified tomato, carrot, apple, cabbage and corn GLB models and their colormap
from Kenney Food Kit 2.0: https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit

License: CC0. The original License.txt is included.

Tomatoes are used by farm crops, shop shelves and checkout. Other models currently
decorate the seed-stall produce display; they are not implemented crop types.
