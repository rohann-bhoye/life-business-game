# Kenney Food Kit Models

Creator: Kenney (https://kenney.nl).
Pack: Food Kit 2.0, released 26 June 2024.
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit
License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0).

Included files: soda-bottle.glb, bag.glb and their Textures/colormap.png dependency,
unmodified from the official pack.
These assets may be used for personal, educational and commercial purposes.
The game fits them to its shelf dimensions at runtime. Inventory identifiers,
prices, save data and checkout interactions remain independent of visual models.
